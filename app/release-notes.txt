New field BIKE LINK: the state of the link (LINKED, BIKE OFF, NO BOARD, BT OFF, NO PERM); tap it for what to do. Fields orange for a warning (bike off, board battery low), red for an error. If the Karoo turns Bluetooth off at START (no Bluetooth LE sensor paired), BoschData now tells you: BT OFF on the field and an alert once per ride. Fix: pair a Bluetooth LE sensor and keep it in the profile.
