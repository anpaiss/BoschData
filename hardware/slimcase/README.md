# Slim case for the BEStie board (Feather nRF52840 Express + LiPo 500 mAh)

Two snap-fit shells, no screws, no standoffs: the board and the battery are held by double-sided foam tape
(floor → tape → LiPo → tape → Feather). Generated by `slimcase.py` (axis-aligned boxes → watertight STL).

- `slim-case-bottom.stl` 59.9 × 33.0 × 18.5 mm, 11.2 cm³
- `slim-case-lid.stl` 59.9 × 33.0 × 4.5 mm, 3.6 cm³ (printed as is: plate down, skirt up)
- assembled 59.9 × 33.0 × 20 mm; inner pocket 53.9 × 27 × 17 mm; walls 3 mm
- USB end: 20 mm opening above the board level for the charging cable, the 2.5 mm gap behind the board is for the LiPo wires
- the LiPo goes under the USB half of the board, the antenna end stays free
- lid: 1.5 mm skirt that drops inside the pocket (long sides + antenna end), 0.4 mm ridge at its tip snapping into a
  groove in the pocket wall 1.5 mm below the rim; sized for MJF PA12 (Weerg), 0.15 mm fit
- nothing thinner than 1.5 mm anywhere (`thickness.py` ray-casts every face; Weerg flags features under ~1 mm,
  it flagged a 0.9 mm wall, a 0.5 mm ridge sliver and a 0.8 mm lip in earlier versions)

`python slimcase.py out` regenerates the STLs, `python render.py preview.png out/*.stl` checks they are watertight and draws
them, `python thickness.py out/slim-case-bottom.stl 1.45` lists every spot thinner than the threshold.

Printed by PolyD in Nylon FAST (MJF PA12), delivered 10/11/2026: the lid closes, but hard. The 0.4 mm ridge on three
sides with FIT 0.15 means about 0.25 mm of interference per side against stiff 3 mm walls. For a reprint: ridge
0.25 mm on the long sides only (none on the antenna end), FIT 0.2.
