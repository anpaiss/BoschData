# Slim case for the BEStie board (Feather nRF52840 Express + LiPo 500 mAh)

Two snap-fit shells, no screws, no standoffs: the board and the battery are held by double-sided foam tape
(floor → tape → LiPo → tape → Feather). Generated by `slimcase.py` (axis-aligned boxes → watertight STL).

- `slim-case-bottom.stl` 60.7 × 31.8 × 18.5 mm, 11.5 cm³
- `slim-case-lid.stl` 60.7 × 31.8 × 4.5 mm, 3.5 cm³ (printed upside down, skirt up)
- assembled 60.7 × 31.8 × 20 mm; walls 3.4 mm; inner pocket 53.9 × 25 × 17 mm
- USB end: 20 mm opening above the board level for the charging cable, the 2.5 mm gap behind the board is for the LiPo wires
- the LiPo goes under the USB half of the board, the antenna end stays free
- lid: 1.2 mm skirt in a flush rebate with a 0.5 mm snap ridge (thinnest material 1.55 mm at the groove: Weerg flagged 1.15 mm) on the long sides; sized for MJF PA12 (Weerg), 0.15 mm fit

`python slimcase.py out` regenerates the STLs, `python render.py preview.png out/*.stl` checks they are watertight and draws them.
